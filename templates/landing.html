{# Tour Stage 1: Ingestion & Format #}
STAGE 1

Automated Ingestion & 12-Digit APAAR Format Check

Institutions onboard student batches via bulk CSV or manual registration. Every record undergoes strict server-side validation against the national identifier standard: exactly 12 numeric digits (^\d{12}$).

  • Zero PII Leakage: APAAR references are automatically masked (e.g. XXXXXXXX9012).
  • Duplicate Detection: Pre-checks verify that the identifier does not collide with existing active student records.
  • Idempotent Processing: Re-uploading identical student rows updates existing records without duplicating ledger slots.
Deterministic Mock Adapter: Validation strictly adheres to format verification. Not connected to live UIDAI or government systems.

{# Tour Stage 2: Dynamic Seat Ledger (PREVENT) #}
STAGE 2 • PREVENT

Dynamic Seat Ledger: Intake Cap Enforcement

Prevents ghost enrolments and unauthorized intake expansion in real time. The Dynamic Seat Ledger guarantees that approved intake caps (e.g. 120 regular seats) are strictly enforced at the database layer with atomic locking.

  • Quota Segregation: Regular seats and Supernumerary quotas (EWS/TFWS/FN) are strictly separated. Supernumerary slots cannot be used to absorb excess regular admissions.
  • Atomic Concurrency: Race conditions on the 120th seat are resolved atomically.
  • Zero Over-Admission: The 121st regular admission attempt is immediately blocked with error code CAPACITY_EXCEEDED.
Immediate Regulatory Feedback: Rejections are logged directly into the audit trail and flagged as excess-intake violations.

{# Tour Stage 3: Identity Graph (DETECT) #}
STAGE 3 • DETECT

Identity Graph & Explainable Risk Engine

When academic records are submitted across multiple colleges, the Identity Graph correlates APAAR references, normalized names, contact records, and fee-payer references to detect conflicts and fraudulent double-enrolments.

  • Deterministic Rules: Scores are calculated with transparent point weights (e.g. +40 for Duplicate APAAR, +35 for Simultaneous Enrolment, capped at 100).
  • Zero "Black Box" AI: Every score includes full explainability so regulators can review the exact evidence chain.
  • Human-in-the-Loop: High-risk cases are escalated for manual review. No student is ever automatically penalized or cancelled without human adjudication.
Investigation Signals: Shared phone numbers or fee references are treated as investigative corroboration, never as automatic rejection proof.

{# Tour Stage 4: Academic Passport (PROVE) #}
STAGE 4 • PROVE

Academic Passport: Cryptographic Credential Governance

Upon completion or milestone achievement, institutions issue tamper-proof academic credentials signed with Ed25519 asymmetric cryptography. Students hold their Academic Passport and can selectively share credentials with employers.

  • Offline QR Verification: Third-party verifiers can authenticate degree authenticity instantly via QR without needing API access or database lookups.
  • Time-Bounded Sharing: Students generate consent-governed sharing tokens with expiring windows (24h, 72h, or 168h).
  • Selective Disclosure: Verifiers only see agreed-upon claims (e.g. Degree Authenticity and Institution), keeping full APAAR and sensitive personal data private.
Zero Heavy Chains: Uses standard, lightweight Ed25519 public-key signatures. Completely offline-capable, green, and verifiable anywhere.

{# The Three Pillars Showcase #}

The Three Architecture Pillars

Core mechanisms engineered to solve the SIH PS 26263 requirements without buzzwords or black boxes.

PILLAR 1

Dynamic Seat Ledger

Guarantees that admission numbers can never exceed AICTE sanctioned capacity limits for any programme or academic session.

  • Maintains strict isolation between Regular and Supernumerary intakes.
  • Database-level row locking resolves concurrency on the final seat.
  • Every attempted excess admission generates a regulatory audit signal.
PILLAR 2

Identity Graph & Risk

A multi-factor identity correlation engine detecting ghost students, duplicate APAARs, and cross-state simultaneous admissions.

  • Deterministic rule weighting capped at 100 with full auditability.
  • Shared contact numbers and fee references tracked as investigative signals.
  • Human-in-the-loop: Flags for review; never auto-penalizes.
PILLAR 3

Academic Passport

Cryptographic credential issuance providing tamper-evident degree verification for students, institutions, and employers.

  • Ed25519 digital signatures signed by the issuing institution.
  • Server-generated QR verification without client-side key exposure.
  • Consent-governed time-bounded sharing tokens (24h/72h/168h).
