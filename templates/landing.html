About APAARSetu

APAARSetu serves as the digital bridge connecting students, institutions, and regulatory authorities into a cohesive, verified academic framework. By anchoring institutional admissions to unique academic identifiers, the platform guarantees that each student's educational trajectory is permanently recorded, validated, and protected against duplication.

The system simplifies credit recognition, enforces sanctioned intake caps in real time, and streamlines seamless transitions between academic institutions across India.

01. Student Onboarding & Format Validation

STAGE 1

Approved institutions submit student admission cohorts via bulk CSV or manual registration. Every record undergoes strict validation against the 12-digit numeric format rule (^\d{12}$) and is automatically masked for personal privacy.

Result: Invalid or malformed identifiers are immediately detected before admission commits.
{# Card 2 #}

02. Dynamic Seat Ledger Capacity Check

STAGE 2 • PREVENT

The Dynamic Seat Ledger verifies sanctioned intake caps in real time. In a programme with 120 sanctioned regular seats, admission #120 succeeds; any subsequent attempt to admit student #121 is blocked with a CAPACITY_EXCEEDED rejection.

Result: Eliminates ghost admissions and strictly preserves supernumerary quota isolation.
{# Card 3 #}

03. Identity Graph & Cross-College Conflict Resolution

STAGE 3 • DETECT

Cross-institutional scanning matches academic accounts across colleges. Simultaneous enrolments (e.g. active full-time courses at VESIT and COEP) trigger explainable risk points (+40 duplicate ID, +35 simultaneous course).

Human Review: An investigation case is opened for regulatory officers. No automatic student cancellation.
{# Card 4 #}

04. Academic Passport & Cryptographic QR Proof

STAGE 4 • PROVE

Upon graduation or milestone completion, institutions sign academic credentials using Ed25519 asymmetric cryptography. Students receive verifiable digital certificates readable via QR code without database lookups.

Result: Third-party employers and verifiers authenticate degrees in milliseconds offline.
